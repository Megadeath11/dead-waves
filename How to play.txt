DEAD WAVES
==========
Double-click DeadWaves.exe to play. Nothing else to install.

Solo works on its own. For co-op, have Steam open before you launch the game.
(It runs on Steam's free "Spacewar" test app, 480, so Steam shows you as playing Spacewar.)

Co-op: everyone needs DeadWaves.exe. One player clicks "Host co-op (Steam)" then
"Invite friends" or "Copy code". The others open the game first, then accept the invite
or click "Join co-op game" and paste the code.

Controls (defaults - change any key under Settings > Key bindings)
  WASD move, Shift sprint, Ctrl/C crouch, Space jump
  Left mouse fire, Right mouse aim (no spread: shots go exactly where the dot is)
  R reload, 1/2/Q/mouse wheel switch gun, 3 wind charge, V knife, G grenade
  4 third gun (Mule Kick), Enter vote to skip the break between rounds
  E buy / open doors / ride elevators / hold to revive, Tab scoreboard, Esc menu, F11 fullscreen
  T place a sentry turret (8,000, up to 4 each), Tab players online with stats and ping
  Jumping headshots are crits (double damage).
  Getting revived keeps your perks; your guns drop one Pack-a-Poonch tier.
  Pack-a-Poonch tiers also double the gun's magazine and ammo each time.
  Dodgers (hooded, cyan eyes, from wave 6) sidestep when you aim at them: shoot between dodges.

Settings: graphics presets (Low to Ultra) plus render resolution, shadows, FPS limit
and an FPS counter; separate music / effects volume; aim sensitivity, invert Y,
toggle aim / crouch; crosshair colour and size; HUD size; view bob and screen shake.
If the game runs slowly, pick Low or turn Render resolution down.

If Windows SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info"
then "Run anyway" - the exe isn't code-signed.
